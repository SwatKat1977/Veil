# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and community members pledge to make participation in the Veil community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Veil is a collaborative platform built around creativity, storytelling, and shared experiences. We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Helping new contributors learn and participate
- Respecting boundaries during discussions and collaborative roleplay-related development
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission
- Deliberate intimidation or exclusion of contributors
- Sexualised language or imagery outside clearly appropriate contexts
- Disruptive behaviour intended to derail discussions or collaboration
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting

## Roleplay and Creative Content

Veil supports tabletop gaming experiences that may explore difficult, mature, or fictional themes. However:

- Fictional content does not excuse abusive behaviour toward real people
- Contributors and community members must maintain respect for one another at all times
- Discussions around game systems, themes, and storytelling should remain constructive and considerate

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories
- Issue trackers
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Community chat spaces
- Documentation and wiki pages
- Public and private communications related to the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the Veil project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of reporters wherever possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

1. Warning the offender
2. Temporary suspension from community spaces
3. Removal of content
4. Permanent bans from the project or community spaces

The severity of consequences will depend on the nature and pattern of the behaviour.
